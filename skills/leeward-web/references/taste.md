# Taste

Leeward sites don't all look alike. Each project picks one **feel**, and the feel sets type, colour,
density and motion. The owner's hard rules in `anti-slop.md` hold for every feel. Beyond them, every
site leads with the photo or the product, keeps very little text above the fold, writes real details
(times, capacities, inclusions, places) plainly, and moves calmly.

## Feels

Pick one from the brief and the client's brand, and say which. Don't mix.

| Feel | When | Type | Colour | Density & layout | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Premium** | Yachts, fine dining, villas, boutique hotels | Refined serif or elegant sans for display, quiet sans body; generous size, tight leading | Near-monochrome, deep tone + one muted accent | Lots of space, big full-bleed photography, few words per screen | Slow, subtle fades; nothing bouncy |
| **Playful** | Tours, beach bars, water sports, family activities | Friendly rounded or geometric sans (not a novelty font) | Brighter accent from the place or brand, still one | Looser rhythm, tilted or overlapping photos allowed, short punchy copy | A little more life: hover lifts, small reveals |
| **Simple** | Small local businesses, one-page sites, a single offer | One clean sans | Neutral + brand accent | One page, short sections, booking one tap away | Minimal |
| **In-depth** | Many products, routes, menus, rooms | Highly legible sans, clear heading hierarchy | Neutral + accent for wayfinding | Detail pages, filters or tabs, FAQ, comparison tables | Minimal; speed matters more |
| **Cinematic** *(proposed)* | Premium clients who want the site to feel like a film | Big type over moving imagery | Often dark | Full-bleed video or very large photography, slow scroll-driven reveals | Strong `visual_effects` allowed, but only with a poster, a pause control, a static reduced-motion version and the speed budget met |

Reel Charters is **premium leaning in-depth**: Fraunces + Manrope, navy and ocean palette, photo-led
hero with no buttons, detail pages per boat and island, booking prefilled from every card.

## Reference sites the owner likes

Studied 2026-11-07 (screenshots at 1440px and 390px, styles measured in the browser).

| Site | Feel | What to take from it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Tres Rotterdam](https://www.tresrotterdam.com/) | Premium (restaurant) | Full-bleed video hero with **nothing on it** but a small floating nav holding the logo and one "Reserve" button. One quiet italic line in a corner and a tracked uppercase season label. Playfair Display / Source Sans 3. Warm off-white `#f6f3ee`, brown-black ink `#38332e`. Confidence through restraint. |
| [Apple](https://www.apple.com/) | Premium / simple | The product photo *is* the hero. Product name + one short line, centred. One family (SF Pro), semibold, tight tracking, 17px body. Black, white, grey and one blue `#0071e3`. Every section repeats one pattern, so a long page stays calm. |
| [Google Antigravity](https://antigravity.google/) | Simple (tech) | White page, huge 80px headline at **regular weight (450)** with tight tracking; one Download button; a faint particle field as the only decoration (it's the product's identity). Google Sans Flex, near-black `#121317`. Big without being loud. |
| [Leeward Digital](https://www.leewarddigital.com/) | Leeward's own (bold) | Very large bold headline (~120px, about −4% tracking), two words in the accent; a small who-and-where line above ("Brand growth studio · St. Maarten & worldwide"); a one-sentence subline. Navy `#0a1a3a`. |
| [ZapCat Tours](https://zapcattours.vercel.app/) | Playful (Leeward build) | Full-bleed action photo with people on the boats. Barlow Condensed 800 uppercase headline, tracked uppercase eyebrow, **square** yellow `#ffc400` buttons, GPS coordinates in a corner. Energy from photo and type, not effects. |
| Reel Charters (Leeward) | Premium, in-depth | Photo-led hero with no buttons, booking prefilled from every card, WhatsApp fallback when the form fails. |

**What they share:** one photo, product or video does the hero's work; one family, or one display +
one body; one accent; little copy above the fold; a big headline with tight tracking.

**Where they break the owner's rules (don't copy):** pill buttons (Tres, Apple, Antigravity, Leeward);
two hero buttons (Apple, Leeward, ZapCat); Leeward's soft blue glow and ZapCat's orange script accent.
